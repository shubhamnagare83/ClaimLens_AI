CLAIMLENS MOTOR SECURE POLICY
=============================
Policy ID: CMSP-2026
Version: 2026.1
Effective: 01 January 2026
Issuer: ClaimLens Insurance Ltd.

SECTION 1: ELIGIBILITY

POL-001 — Policy Validity
Coverage applies only when the incident occurs during the active policy period.
The policy start date and end date define the coverage window.
Any incident occurring before the policy start date or after the policy end date is not covered.

POL-007 — Vehicle Registration Requirement
The insured vehicle must have a valid registration certificate (RC) issued by the Regional Transport Office.
The registration number on the policy must match the vehicle registration number on all submitted documents.

POL-008 — Valid Driving Licence
At the time of an accident, the insured vehicle must be driven by a person holding a valid and effective
driving licence appropriate for the type of vehicle. A car requires a Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) licence.
A two-wheeler requires a two-wheeler licence or LMV licence.

POL-020 — Vehicle Ownership Verification
The claimant must be the registered owner of the insured vehicle or an authorized representative.
The name on the policy must match the name on the vehicle registration certificate.

POL-021 — Policy Transfer
If the vehicle has been transferred to a new owner, the insurance policy must also be transferred
within 14 days of the vehicle transfer. Claims made by a new owner without policy transfer will not be covered.


SECTION 2: COVERAGE

POL-002 — Own Damage Coverage
Own damage coverage provides compensation for damage to the insured vehicle caused by accidents,
collisions, overturning, fire, explosion, natural calamities, or malicious acts by third parties.
The claim amount is subject to the Insured Declared Value (IDV) and applicable deductibles.

POL-003 — Theft Coverage
Theft coverage provides compensation for the loss of the insured vehicle due to theft or attempted theft.
The insured must file a First Information Report (FIR) with the police within 24 hours of discovering the theft.
Coverage is limited to the Insured Declared Value (IDV) at the time of theft.

POL-004 — Accident Coverage
Accident coverage compensates for damage to the insured vehicle resulting from road accidents,
collisions with other vehicles, objects, or animals, and single-vehicle accidents such as overturning.

POL-022 — Geographic Coverage
The policy provides coverage within the geographical boundaries of India.
Incidents occurring outside India are not covered unless specifically endorsed.


SECTION 3: CLAIM WINDOW

POL-005 — Claim Notification Window
Claims should normally be reported to the insurer within 7 calendar days of the incident date.
Reasonable delay may be considered when supported by evidence such as hospitalization,
remote location, or other justifiable circumstances.

POL-030 — Reasonable Delay Exception
If the claim is reported beyond the standard 7-day window, the insurer may still consider the claim
if the policyholder provides reasonable justification for the delay.


SECTION 4: DOCUMENTATION

POL-006 — Required Claim Documents
Every claim submission must include a duly completed claim form.
For accident claims: incident description and repair estimate.
For theft claims: FIR, vehicle registration certificate, and key declaration.

POL-016 — Theft FIR Requirement
For theft claims, a First Information Report (FIR) filed with the local police is mandatory.
The FIR must be filed within 24 hours of discovering the theft.
Claims without an FIR will not be processed.

POL-017 — Theft Key Requirement
For theft claims, the policyholder must submit all sets of vehicle keys to the insurer
along with a signed key declaration.

POL-018 — Repair Estimate Requirement
For accident and own damage claims, a detailed repair estimate from an authorized workshop is required.

POL-019 — Repair Invoice Requirement
After repairs are completed, the policyholder must submit the final repair invoice for reimbursement.

POL-023 — Accident Evidence Requirements
For accident claims, the policyholder must provide a detailed description of the accident
including date, time, location, circumstances, and parties involved.

POL-024 — Theft Reporting Requirements
Theft must be reported to the police immediately and to the insurer within the claim notification window.

POL-025 — Police Acknowledgement
For incidents involving third parties, significant property damage, or injuries,
a police report or acknowledgement may be required.

POL-033 — Documentation Accuracy
All submitted documents must be consistent with each other and with the facts of the incident.
Material inconsistencies between documents may require clarification or investigation.


SECTION 5: EXCLUSIONS

POL-009 — Driving Licence Exclusion
No coverage if the vehicle was being driven by a person not holding a valid and effective driving licence.

POL-010 — Alcohol and Substance Exclusion
No coverage if the driver was under the influence of alcohol or any intoxicating substance.

POL-011 — Intentional Damage Exclusion
No coverage for loss or damage caused intentionally by the insured.

POL-012 — Wear and Tear Exclusion
No coverage for damage resulting from normal wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, or corrosion.

POL-013 — Mechanical or Electrical Breakdown
No coverage for loss due to mechanical or electrical breakdown unless directly caused by an accident.

POL-032 — Misrepresentation
If any claim is found to be based on misrepresentation or fraudulent documentation,
the insurer reserves the right to deny the claim.

POL-036 — Commercial Use Exclusion
If the insured vehicle is registered as private but was being used for commercial purposes,
coverage may be denied.

POL-038 — Modification Exclusion
Damage to or caused by unauthorized modifications not declared in the policy are excluded.


SECTION 6: VALUATION

POL-014 — Insured Declared Value (IDV)
The IDV represents the maximum sum insured for the vehicle.
The total claim amount cannot exceed the IDV.

POL-015 — Deductible
A compulsory deductible applies to every claim.
Cars: Rs. 2,000. Two-wheelers: Rs. 1,000.

POL-026 — Total Loss Assessment
If the cost of repair exceeds 75% of the IDV, the vehicle may be declared a total loss.

POL-027 — Partial Loss Assessment
For partial loss, the insurer will pay repair costs subject to depreciation, deductible, and IDV limits.

POL-028 — Salvage Rights
In case of total loss or theft recovery, the insurer has the right to take possession of the salvage.

POL-029 — Depreciation Schedule
Depreciation is applied to replaced parts based on vehicle age.
Rubber, nylon, plastic: 50%. Fiberglass: 30%. Glass: nil. Paint: 50%.


SECTION 7: PROCESS

POL-031 — Claim Investigation Rights
The insurer reserves the right to investigate any claim through appointed surveyors or investigators.

POL-034 — Human Review Requirement
All claim recommendations are advisory. Final decisions must be made by authorized human investigators.

POL-035 — Dispute Resolution
In case of dispute, the policyholder may request re-evaluation within 30 days.

POL-037 — Multiple Claims
Cumulative claim amount across all claims cannot exceed the IDV.
